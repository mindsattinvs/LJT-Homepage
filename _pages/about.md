I am a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

My research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
